Sunil Kumar

Teacher and owner

I started teaching in this neighbourhood because my younger cousin was failing Maths. I sat with him on our terrace every evening, and by the next exam he was scoring in the seventies. His friends heard about it, and soon ten children were sitting on our floor.

Today the batches are bigger, but my rule has not changed. I will not move to the next topic until the child can explain this one back to me in their own words.

Come and sit in one class. If you do not like it, you pay nothing.
